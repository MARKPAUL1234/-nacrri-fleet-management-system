NaCRRI Institutional Privacy Policy

Information Governance and Motor Register Data Protection Standards

1. Asset Data Governance

The NaCRRI Fleet Management System records motor asset details, location movements, custodianship assignments, and service history strictly for institutional oversight at Namulonge and partner stations.

2. Custodian & User Records

Custodian names, contact references, and administrative log entries are stored securely in internal system state. Personal data is never shared externally or processed for non-institutional purposes.

3. Audit Trail & Data Retention

All service logs and location updates maintain explicit timestamped records. Data retention complies with National Agricultural Research Organisation (NARO) asset management standards.

Institutional Terms & Conditions

Vehicle Usage Protocol and Institutional Asset Governance Rules

1. Motor Operation & Custody

All vehicles, motorcycles, tractors, vans, and buses registered under NaCRRI must be operated exclusively by authorized custodians with valid log entries and approved deployment dates.

2. Mandatory Service Logging

Designated custodians and workshop personnel are obligated to log odometer readings, maintenance completions, or pending service reasons immediately upon status changes.

3. Authorized Access Control

System roles (Super Admin, Programme Admin, Management Viewer) must strictly adhere to assigned administrative scopes. Unauthorized modification of vehicle logs is prohibited.
